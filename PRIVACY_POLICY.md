# Privacy Policy for Darshan Assist

**Effective Date:** July 19, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 19, 2026  

## Overview
Darshan Assist ("we", "our", or "extension") is a productivity Chrome Extension and Web Dashboard designed to assist Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) pilgrims with form autofilling, release countdown reminders, and travel planning.

We believe in complete transparency and operate on a strict **Local-First Architecture**. Your personal data remains 100% under your control on your device.

---

## 1. Information We Process
Darshan Assist allows users to voluntarily save family pilgrim profiles (such as Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, ID Card Type, ID Card Number, State, and Contact Mobile/Email). 

* **Local Storage:** All profile information is stored locally on your device's browser storage (IndexedDB and Chrome Local Storage) using AES-256 encryption.
* **No Server Uploads:** We do NOT collect, transmit, upload, or store any of your personal data or identity documents on any external server or database.

---

## 2. Chrome Permissions & Usage
* **`storage`**: Used to persist your encrypted family profiles, release calendar events, and notification preferences locally on your browser.
* **`alarms` & `notifications`**: Used to schedule and trigger desktop reminder alerts before TTD ticket release times.
* **`activeTab` & `scripting`**: Used to detect form fields on official TTD booking pages (`ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in`) to enable 1-click form autofill when triggered by the user.

---

## 3. Data Protection & Security
* **AES-256 Encryption:** Sensitive fields (such as ID card numbers and phone numbers) are encrypted on your device.
* **No Tracking:** We do not track your browsing history, IP address, web activity, or financial transactions.

---

## 4. Third-Party Links & Affiliates
The extension's Trip Planner section may contain optional third-party links to travel services (such as bus, train, hotel, or map resources). Clicking these external links will redirect you to third-party websites governed by their respective privacy policies.

---

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our official support channels or project repository.
